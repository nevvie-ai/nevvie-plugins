Work by the `guide` skill: one question at a time, nothing goes out without their yes, no tech words.

# Follow-up emails

1. **Welcome:** sent by the ticket channel with the order confirmation (on Hipsy: the event's
   `settings/communication` text): `emails/welcome.md` filled for this event (date, times, the
   recipe's bring list, anything moved out of the public listing). Set it during the ticket channel's publish step.
2. **Reminder, thank-you, extra message:** to everyone with a ticket, through the ticket
   channel's message-all function (Hipsy: *E-mail Deelnemers*), or as drafts in their Gmail if connected and
   personal emails are needed. Marketing only to people who opted in to the newsletter.
3. Write with the `voice` skill. Show one filled example and the number of people; act only on their yes.
4. Never paste lists of names or emails into the chat or the folder.
