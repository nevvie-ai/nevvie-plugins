# Follow-up emails

1. Template from `emails/` (welcome, reminder, thank-you), filled per person with first name, event,
   date, doors and start time, ticket type, and the recipe's "What to bring".
2. Who gets it: participants of that event, from Hipsy (connection, or the `Exporteer` CSV the
   owner downloads; read it where it is, never save it into the folder). Marketing messages only to
   people who opted in to the newsletter.
3. Show one filled example and the number of people. On yes: create the emails as drafts in
   their Gmail (if connected) for them to send, or use Hipsy's `E-mail Deelnemers` for one message
   to everyone.
4. Never paste lists of names or emails into the chat.
