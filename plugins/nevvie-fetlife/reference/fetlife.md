# FetLife: what's allowed (read 2026-10-28)

Sources: FetLife Terms of Use (`fetlife.com/legal/terms-of-use-hwfvu`) and FetLife Rules &
Policies overview (`fetlife.com/policies`). Re-read them when in doubt; they change.

## How Claude works with FetLife

- **Claude never operates or reads FetLife automatically.** The terms forbid automated means
  (bots, robots, crawlers, scripts) to download data or create accounts, and collecting members'
  personal data for commercial purposes. So this channel **prepares the event ready to paste**,
  field by field, and **the owner posts it herself**. She pastes the link back; Claude records it.
- Never ask for, copy or store RSVP lists, member names or messages from FetLife.

## Rules check (gate before she posts)

1. **Kink-related:** the business and the event relate to kink, fetish, BDSM or leather (a profile
   or event for anything else isn't allowed).
2. **A community event, not a business promotion:** FetLife's event section doesn't allow
   personal ads or business promotions. Write it as an event for the community: what happens, for
   whom, how consent works; tickets as practical information, not a sales pitch.
3. **One listing per event:** no duplicate listings, no "recycled" events (re-posting an old
   event as new). A new date is a new event; never post the same event twice.
4. **No spam:** the same message not more than 3 times a day across groups; no unsolicited
   promotion outside areas meant for it.
5. **Off-site links:** a ticket link is fine as practical information; the account must genuinely
   take part in the community and not exist only to send people off-site. Accounts younger than
   three months may not promote at all.
6. **Content:** explicit kink content is allowed, unlike on Hipsy, but only material the owner
   made or has permission to share (photos of participants only with their consent), nothing
   involving minors, no illegal drugs or controlled substances, nothing hateful.
7. **No professional sessions:** no events to promote a session with a professional dominant or
   submissive or a phone sex operator.

Verdict: "Ready for FetLife" or "Not yet: <n> open points". She posts only after "Ready".
